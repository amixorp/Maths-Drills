🔺 Number Pyramids

Every brick is the sum of the two bricks directly below it. The bottom row is given — pupils fill in the empty bricks above. Addition only, no subtraction.
Numbers stay small: the bottom row uses 1–9 (1–6 for 4-row pyramids) and no brick ever goes above 30, so every step is a gentle mental addition.
Reveal answers fills the blanks in on screen — they are never printed. The Worksheet PDF and Answers PDF are separate two-page files. Add ?seed=12345 to reproduce a sheet, or ?seed=12345&answers=1 to open it already revealed.
