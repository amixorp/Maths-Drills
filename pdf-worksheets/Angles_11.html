📐 Angles

Five bands, in equal measure: straight-line angles → angles round a point → triangles (including valid exterior-angle questions) → isosceles triangles → parallel lines.
Rare right-angled isosceles triangles are marked with a square and need no given angle values.
All angles are whole degrees; the first two isosceles questions use round, easy-to-calculate values.
Triangles are randomly rotated and scaled to fit neatly inside their boxes.
Reveal answers shows them inline on screen — they're never printed.
